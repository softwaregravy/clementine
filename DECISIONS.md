# Decisions

Provenance log for Clementine, consolidated on 2026-09-19 from the PRD changelogs (v0.2 → v0.9), the Phase 1 design doc (v0.2), and the working sessions behind them. Going forward, append entries here in the same commit as the change they justify.

**Status vocabulary.** *Decided* — John explicitly confirmed it. *Proposed* — Claude proposed it and John has neither confirmed nor vetoed; it does not bind. *Superseded* — retired by a later entry, kept so the arc stays readable. Where a decision's text lives in the PRD, the entry says so (PRD vX, §n). Design-doc IDs (D1–D7, P1–P20, A1–A4) are referenced where they apply.

IDs are chronological within each section.

## 1. Product identity and scope

- **DEC-001 · 2026-07 (pre-PRD) · Build a multi-user, invite-only system for family and friends — under 20 users in year one.** Decided. Useful to John first; small enough to run from a console; no self-serve, no billing, no marketing. (PRD §3)
- **DEC-002 · 2026-07 · Parking violations only.** Decided. Camera violations (school-zone speed, red light, bus lane) are out of scope; the job stays type-aware so enabling them later is configuration, not migration. (PRD §4)
- **DEC-003 · 2026-07 · Zero dispute support.** Decided. A ticket the user doesn't want to pay is never authorized; Phase 2's dismiss silences it. The product takes no position on disputes. (PRD §4)
- **DEC-004 · 2026-07 · No autonomous payment, ever.** Decided, reversing an earlier fully-automatic idea. Every payment requires explicit, logged user authorization. Granularity refined by DEC-023. (PRD §6 Phase 3)
- **DEC-005 · 2026-07-30 · Name: Clementine (from PlateWatch).** Decided. "Watch" reads surveillance-adjacent in the ALPR/Flock climate; "ticket" words conflate with events. The brainstorm anchored on the orange envelope, the street-parking user base, and ease of payment. Collision scan 2026-07-30: nothing in the parking/vehicle/government-payment space; a UK sleep app holds the iOS name and clementineapp.com; a French accounting fintech was acquired in 2025. Run a real trademark search before anything public-facing; `clementine.nyc` noted as a fitting domain. (PRD v0.3)
- **DEC-006 · 2026-08-02 · Agent, not processor.** Decided. Clementine never holds or moves funds; it submits the user's payment details into CityPay checkout as their agent; funds flow user → NYC DOF. Vocabulary swept: "submits payment on the user's behalf," "confirmation of actions taken" (never "receipt"). (PRD v0.4, §2)
- **DEC-007 · 2026-08-05 · Purpose: a learning vehicle that earns its keep.** Decided. Primarily an exercise in AI-assisted product development by its author, justified by being useful to the author and an invited circle. Not commercial. Competitors in the category are read as market validation, not threat. (PRD v0.7, §2)
- **DEC-008 · 2026-08-05 · Solicited by design, defined at the subscription level.** Decided, in John's words. Subscribing *is* the request to receive messages; solicited does not mean user-initiated per message; nobody is enrolled who didn't ask, so nothing Clementine sends is unsolicited — the category does not exist in this system. Vocabulary: system-timed sends are "scheduled," never "unsolicited." Testable invariant: 0 sends without a live subscription. (PRD v0.7–v0.8, §2, §9)
- **DEC-009 · 2026-08-05 · Clementine never borrows CityPay's name, branding or look.** Decided, as the scam-adjacency mitigation. DOF publicly warns that unsolicited parking-payment texts are fraud; every send is solicited, arrives from one known number, and links only to the canonical CityPay URL (Phase 1) or our own page (Phase 2). (PRD v0.7, §10)
- **DEC-010 · 2026-08-05 · Phase 3 scope re-examination deferred.** Decided. Phase 3 is re-examined against solicited-by-design, scam adjacency and the landscape findings when it specs up; nothing changes before then. (PRD §11)

## 2. Phasing

- **DEC-011 · 2026-07-29 · Three-deliverable rollout: D1 SMS with CityPay link → D2 in-app page → D3 confirm-all.** *Superseded* by DEC-012. (PRD v0.2)
- **DEC-012 · 2026-08-02 · Phases are the ships: Text → Page → Pay.** Decided. The separate deliverables axis merged into phases. "Phase 0" was rejected: nothing in Phase 1 is throwaway; the cron → classify → SMS pipeline is the permanent engine. (PRD v0.4, §6)
- **DEC-013 · 2026-08-02 · Phase 1 is a stateless daily digest.** Decided. One table (`subscriptions`: phone, plate, state, nickname), one daily job, no ticket memory, no send markers, no accounts, no UI. Cut from Phase 1: violations mirror, send dedup, login, email capture, backfill, lag instrumentation, monitoring alerts. Accepted noise: the same open ticket re-texts daily until satisfied; a retry after a partial send can double-text. Ends with Phase 2 memory. (PRD v0.4, §6)
- **DEC-014 · 2026-08-02 · Daily cadence, 24-hour notification SLA.** Decided. One poll per plate per day, immediately before the send. Closed the polling-cadence question v0.2 had deferred pending lag data; lag instrumentation died with it. Retained on 2026-08-05 even though new tickets load weekly, because satisfied tickets clear daily — a daily poll stops the nag within a day of payment. (PRD v0.4, v0.6)
- **DEC-015 · 2026-08-02 · Phase 1 captures phone + plate only.** Decided. Legal name, email and billing address move to Phase 3's payment-profile setup; the first PAY routes to profile setup before text authorization works. (PRD v0.4)
- **DEC-016 · 2026-08-02 · Phone-first auth via SMS OTP; login ships in Phase 2.** Decided (ratified from draft). (PRD v0.4, §6 Phase 2)
- **DEC-017 · 2026-08-02 · Dismiss, refresh (on-login + manual) and admin health alerting are Phase 2.** Decided. Phase 1 observability is logs only; admin operations are the Rails console. (PRD v0.4)
- **DEC-018 · 2026-08-04 · Urgency and overdue messaging deferred to Phase 3.** Decided. Direction sketch on record: "4 are overdue, and 1 more goes overdue in 3 days." Until then: Phase 1 repetition, then the Phase 2 page countdown. (PRD v0.5)

## 3. Messaging and sends

- **DEC-019 · 2026-07-30 · Quiet hours fixed 21:00–09:00 ET, queued-not-dropped; money-movement sends pierce.** *Superseded* by DEC-020. (PRD v0.3)
- **DEC-020 · 2026-08-02 · Quiet hours dissolved.** Decided. The rule survives only as "the daily send sits in waking hours"; Phase 3 post-hoc sends (confirmation, failure, unauthorized-submission alert) go immediately at any hour. (PRD v0.4)
- **DEC-021 · 2026-08-04 · Daily send at ~09:00 ET; no tickets → no text.** Decided (both had been proposed in v0.4). "Silence means clean" promoted from implicit behavior to a stated contract in the welcome message. (PRD v0.5)
- **DEC-022 · 2026-08-04 · Phase 1 digest is count-only.** Decided. "N open parking tickets on [nickname] ([plate]). Pay or view: [link]." No amounts, totals, deadline math or urgency flag in Phase 1 — the digest's job is detection and routing; CityPay renders the detail one tap away. (PRD v0.5)
- **DEC-023 · 2026-08-02 · Phase 3 authorization by text; confirmation is transaction-level.** Decided; supersedes v0.2's link-to-page confirm UX and v0.3's per-ticket confirmation records. The quote SMS itemizes every ticket, amount and total; the user's PAY reply from the registered number is the authorization. One authorization record per transaction with per-ticket line items; amount drift on any ticket aborts the whole transaction and triggers a fresh quote. The separate at-confirm acknowledgment send was deleted; one post-hoc confirmation (SMS + email) per transaction. (PRD v0.4, §6 Phase 3)
- **DEC-024 · 2026-08-04 · Welcome message is Phase 1's second send type.** Decided. Fires at console enrollment: identifies Clementine, echoes plate + nickname, states the contract (morning text when open tickets exist; silence means clean; ingestion lag), STOP/HELP footer. Moves the wrong-plate tripwire from first-ticket time to enrollment time. Sent synchronously from the console (P4, proposed). (PRD v0.5)
- **DEC-025 · 2026-08-04 · Opt-out posture: Twilio-native STOP/HELP.** Decided. A stopped subscriber goes silent and surfaces in send logs; START plus console to resume. (PRD v0.5)
- **DEC-026 · 2026-08-04 · Carrier registration is an M1 prerequisite.** Decided. US application-to-person SMS requires A2P 10DLC brand/campaign registration (or toll-free verification) before production sends; days-to-weeks lead time, so it opens M1. (PRD v0.5, §5)
- **DEC-027 · 2026-08-05 · Number type: A2P 10DLC, local NYC number, sole-proprietor path.** Decided (design doc D6). The digest pattern-matches the ticket-phishing genre; a local number saved as a contact, plus the welcome, is the antidote. PRD amendment A3 (record it in §5) still pending.
- **DEC-028 · 2026-08-05 · Welcome copy says new tickets can take "a week or more" to appear.** Decided. Contract language must match the weekly-loading source. (PRD v0.6)
- **DEC-029 · 2026-08-05 · Alert is the third and last Phase 1 send type.** Decided. Fires on the Uncertain outcome: names the count when it survived the failure, omits it when it didn't, directs the user to the link. Never says "new" — statelessness can't distinguish new from known-open; "open" / "outstanding" only. Final copy lands at campaign registration. (PRD v0.9)

## 4. Data source and read path

- **DEC-030 · 2026-07-29 · CityPay plate search is the data source; probe before parser.** *Superseded* by DEC-032 for Phases 1–2. The Ferrum probe script (`citypay_probe.rb`) saved raw HTML and screenshots deliberately without parsing — a parser written before seeing a real results page is guesswork. (PRD v0.2)
- **DEC-031 · 2026-08-02 · Open Data cut entirely.** *Superseded* by DEC-032. Cut because it had crept into drafts without a decision. (PRD v0.4)
- **DEC-032 · 2026-08-05 · Sole read source for Phases 1–2: NYC Open Data "Open Parking and Camera Violations" (`nc67-uf89`), Socrata SODA API.** Decided, verified live against John's plate. JSON over plain HTTPS, queried by plate + state, app token as a managed secret. CityPay keeps exactly two roles: the digest's payment link and Phase 3's checkout rail. Reinstated as a formal decision with the tradeoff named: new violations load weekly (Sundays), satisfied ones clear daily (Tue–Sun), so worst-case new-ticket detection is ~2 weeks — accepted over CityPay's fresher-but-scrapeable read because a structured public API beats scraping for long-term reliability, and the product was never day-zero. (PRD v0.6, §5)
- **DEC-033 · 2026-08-05 · No browser before Phase 3.** Decided. Phase 1's fetch is a plain HTTPS JSON request; Ferrum/Cuprite (+ Playwright sidecar fallback) are scoped to Phase 3 checkout. The CityPay probe work refiles as Phase 3 recon. Resolves the design doc's probe-gated Q7.1/Q7.4. (PRD v0.6)
- **DEC-034 · 2026-08-05 · Plates normalized at enrollment: uppercase, no spaces or dashes.** Decided — a hard requirement, not a convention. The API matches exactly and case-sensitively; a well-formed query with a lowercase plate returns a legitimate-looking `[]`. (PRD v0.6, §10)
- **DEC-035 · 2026-08-05 · Payment channels re-verified: still no payment API; headless CityPay checkout stands for Phase 3; eCheck is the default stored method.** Decided. Alternatives rejected on record: the mobile app (private undocumented API, adversarial to automate for money movement); the phone line (routes card data through our telephony — processor territory, and no eCheck); programmatic mail (Clementine becomes the account of origin, plus 2–3 weeks of processing); DOF fleet programs (commercial enrollment, trades away hearing rights). (PRD v0.6, §5)
- **DEC-036 · 2026-08-04 · Field needs are phase-tiered.** Decided. P1: enumerable rows + violation-type discrimination. P2: summons number, amount due, issue date. P3: authoritative amounts at execution — from CityPay checkout, never from the lagged dataset. All verified present in the sample on 2026-08-05. (PRD v0.5, v0.9)
- **DEC-037 · 2026-08-05 · Open predicate: `amount_due` cast to integer > 0.** Decided against a live 132-row sample (3 open). `amount_due` is already the net balance; read it directly, never recompute from fine/penalty/interest/reduction/payment. Amounts arrive as strings — the cast is load-bearing. (PRD v0.9, §6)
- **DEC-038 · 2026-08-05 · Camera filter is a camera-side blocklist, not a parking-issuer allowlist.** Decided. Recognized camera rows are excluded; every other row is kept regardless of issuing agency. An allowlist (TRAFFIC + SANITATION) was rejected because it silently drops parking tickets written by other agencies (e.g. NYPD) — a false clean. Seeded from the sample (photo school-zone speed, DOT-issued); extended as red-light/bus-lane examples appear; the exact predicate is a design detail. (PRD v0.9, §4, §6)
- **DEC-039 · 2026-08-05 · Sparse-row disposition.** Decided. A partial row is a well-formed record, never a malformed response. A row with no `amount_due` reads as not-open, is skipped and logged. A financial row that has *lost* `amount_due` on an otherwise-populated response is drift → Uncertain. The tell is universality: one empty row among many is archival noise; every row losing the field is a rename. Misfires err toward "go check," never toward a false clean. (PRD v0.9)

## 5. Phase 1 outcome model and safety

- **DEC-040 · 2026-08-02 · A broken read never reads as "no tickets."** Generalized by DEC-041. Fail loud in logs, skip the day's send; the 24h SLA absorbs a missed day. (PRD v0.4)
- **DEC-041 · 2026-08-05 · Four run outcomes per plate per run: Clean, Count, Uncertain, Unreachable.** Decided, in John's framing: determining *that* tickets exist is a separate, more robust step than reading *what* they are, and the first must never be held hostage to the second. Invariant: **a row we cannot evaluate can never produce a Clean.** Clean → silence; Count → digest; Uncertain → alert, never silent; Unreachable → log, skip, tomorrow covers. §9's "0 false all-clears" broadened to cover failed, malformed or uninterpretable responses. (PRD v0.9, §6, §9)
- **DEC-042 · 2026-08-05 · Retries are scoped to Unreachable only; total unreachability stays silent.** Decided. Re-fetching doesn't fix a changed schema, so Uncertain alerts instead of retrying. No "couldn't check today" send — alerting on every transient blip would train users to ignore the one signal that matters. (PRD v0.9)
- **DEC-043 · 2026-08-05 · Financially-empty rows are skipped silently but logged.** Decided. The log exists to drive enhancements that handle new row shapes. (PRD v0.9)
- **DEC-044 · 2026-08-05 · Residue skip-log at WARN.** Decided. Unclassifiable residue (skipped rows fitting no routine case) logs the full raw row at WARN, tagged subscription id + plate, never phone. Routine paid rows and recognized camera rows are not logged. Daily re-logging under statelessness is accepted at slow scale; WARN denotes a standing condition, not a per-run incident. A run with a logged skip still resolves to Clean or Count. (PRD v0.9, §6, §7)
- **DEC-045 · 2026-08-05 · Log-level hierarchy: INFO routine, WARN residue or a single Unreachable run, ERROR an Uncertain outcome.** *Proposed / derived* — pending John's veto. Residue-at-WARN itself is decided (DEC-044). (PRD v0.9, §7)
- **DEC-046 · 2026-08-05 · Per-plate isolation.** Decided. One plate's Uncertain or Unreachable never affects another plate's run. (PRD v0.9; design doc D5)

## 6. Stack, hosting and tooling (Phase 1 design doc, 2026-08-05)

- **DEC-047 · Decision criteria, ranked: Claude execution strength; John's review fluency; platform continuity; cost at family scale.** Decided (design §1). Technology choices optimize for AI-driven development first; John is strongest in Ruby, with working Python and JavaScript.
- **DEC-048 · Language: Ruby (D1).** Decided.
- **DEC-049 · Rails 8 from day 1 (D2).** Decided. Phase 2 needs it regardless; the console is the admin surface; convention density is an agent asset. Phase 1 routes: Rails' built-in `/up` health check, nothing else.
- **DEC-050 · Hosting: Render for the app's life; AWS is a services catalog, not a destination (D3).** Decided, overriding a proposed Render → AWS migration path. Footprint: web service + cron job + managed Postgres, single region. AWS services are called à la carte only when a requirement names one (KMS at Phase 3; possibly S3 for backups). No planned migration, ever. Earlier (2026-07-29): Render chosen over Railway and Fly.io.
- **DEC-051 · Database: PostgreSQL, Render managed, smallest tier (D4).** Decided. SQLite one-box declined: it couples the app to a disk and spends its savings exactly where Phase 3's audit log and vault live.
- **DEC-052 · Jobs: Render Cron Job + rake task; no job framework in Phase 1 (D5).** Decided, overriding a proposed Solid Queue in-Puma. Sidekiq drags in Redis; Solid Queue arrives at Phase 2, where poll-on-login, refresh and health alerting make it load-bearing. Retry model: in-run retries (~an hour, with backoff); if all fail, skip today, log loudly, tomorrow covers. Trades a small retry-isolation weakness for a real complexity reduction, within the 24h SLA. PRD amendment A2 (replace "Solid Queue or Sidekiq" in §8) still pending.
- **DEC-053 · Tests: RSpec with rspec-rails, factory_bot_rails, webmock (D7); RuboCop + rubocop-rails + rubocop-rspec.** Decided.
- **DEC-054 · 2026-07-29 · Email: Twilio SendGrid, first used in Phase 3.** Decided. (PRD §8)
- **DEC-055 · Proposed batch P1–P20 (design doc, veto-by-exception).** *Proposed* — John has not yet run the veto pass. Summary: GitHub → Render auto-deploy on `main` gated on CI (P1); Render US East (P2); cron at 14:00 UTC (P3); welcome sends synchronously from the console (P4); one Twilio Messaging Service, HELP copy written at registration (P5); unrecognized inbound ignored (P6); no delivery callbacks in Phase 1 (P7); fixture-first parser, WebMock blocks real HTTP (P8); Phase 1 test surface (P9); GitHub Actions rspec + rubocop on every push (P10); live-fire policy — real API only manually from John's machine, real Twilio only to John's phone in dev (P11); `CLAUDE.md` contents (P12); private repo, trunk-based, no PR ceremony (P13); review split — John hand-reviews migrations, anything that sends, and the classifier (P14); secrets as env vars — Render env groups + dotenv (P15); non-secret config as code constants (P16); dev + prod only, no staging (P17); structured JSON logs, every send logged (P18); logs carry subscription id + plate, never phone (P19); no error tracker in Phase 1 (P20). Note: the substance of P18 and P19 was subsequently written into PRD v0.9 §6–§7 as decided.
- **DEC-056 · PRD amendments A1–A4.** A1 (retries within the run) — *landed in substance* in PRD v0.9 §6. A2 (no job framework in Phase 1), A3 (record the 10DLC decision in §5), A4 (~09:00 ET pinned to 14:00 UTC; seasonal drift to 10:00 EDT accepted) — *pending sign-off*; PRD §5/§6/§8 still carry the old wording.

## 7. Phase 3 recon (2026-08-05)

- **DEC-057 · CityPay reference doc replaces the raw HAR/HTML captures.** Decided. `docs/citypay-reference.md` is the canonical record of the lookup POST, session cookie flow, results-table parse map and reCAPTCHA mechanics; the raw captures were dropped once distilled.
- **DEC-058 · reCAPTCHA: document mechanics only; defer the solving strategy.** Decided. Site key, token field, token lifetime and server enforcement recorded; how Clementine obtains a token is left open, with nothing presupposing an answer.
- **DEC-059 · Store both ticket numbers.** Decided. CityPay's internal `VIOLATION_NUMBER` (form/cart key) differs from the summons number printed on the ticket (and used by Open Data). Persist both, keyed on the internal number for CityPay operations; surface the summons number to users.

## 8. Ways of working

- **DEC-060 · 2026-07-29 · Session protocol.** Decided. Claude leads, asks one question at a time, presents alternatives with a recommendation, and seeks one clear answer before moving on.
- **DEC-061 · Each proposed change is explicitly confirmed or vetoed before it hardens.** Decided. Proposed-but-unconfirmed material is flagged separately in changelogs and document bodies; superseded rationale is retired explicitly; open questions are noted honestly, never resolved speculatively.
- **DEC-062 · 2026-08-05 · Probe first, parse second, decide third.** Decided. Investigate real data before writing parsers or making architectural decisions; drop large source files once their essence is in a canonical reference.
- **DEC-063 · 2026-09-19 · Documents move to GitHub; work continues in Claude Code by commits.** Decided. The Claude Project is retired; this repo's `CLAUDE.md` supersedes the project's custom instructions, which still described PlateWatch, per-ticket confirmation, Phase 1 scraping etiquette, and "Solid Queue or Sidekiq."

## Proposed, not yet confirmed — Phase 2/3 material (unchanged since PRD v0.4)

- **Quote supersession:** a newer quote invalidates an unanswered older one; PAY always binds to the most recent quote.
- **Unauthorized submission** = any CityPay submission lacking a matching, valid authorization text (registered number; matching quote; not already executed).
- **Phase 2 internal ship order:** a tokenized read-only results page may ship before OTP; OTP lands with dismiss, the first write worth protecting.
- **SIM-swap posture:** text authorization accepts phone-possession risk at family scale; profile and payment-method changes require the authenticated page, never text.
